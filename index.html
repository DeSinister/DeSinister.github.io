CollideNet

Hierarchical multi-scale video representation learning with disentanglement for time-to-collision forecasting

Hierarchical multi-scale transformer for time-to-collision forecasting in dashcam video. CollideNet disentangles non-stationarity, trend, and seasonality across scales to cut forecasting error and run in real time on GPU. State of the art on CCD, DoTA, and DAD.
